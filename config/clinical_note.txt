"This is a prescription from a clinician:\n"
"History: Patient reports pain in the right hamstring following a sprinting activity 3 days ago.\n"
"Sudden onset of pain during acceleration phase.\n"
"No direct trauma reported.\n"
"Examination: Mild swelling and localized tenderness over the mid-belly of the right hamstring muscle.\n"
"Pain reproduced with resisted knee flexion and passive hip flexion.\n"
"Mild reduction in range of motion due to pain. No palpable defect.\n"
"Strength slightly reduced secondary to pain.\n"
"Neurovascular examination normal.\n"
"Assessment: Grade I right hamstring muscle strain.\n"
"Plan: Relative rest for 1–2 weeks, ice application as needed, compression, gradual return to activity, and referral to physiotherapy for rehabilitation exercises. NSAIDs as required for pain control."
